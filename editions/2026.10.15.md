## ✨ Apps

### [Stayput](https://stayput.dev)
Free file tools for images, PDFs, video and audio that run in the browser: no account, no watermark, and files are not uploaded. After one visit the pages work offline; tools that use a model download it on first use. Open source, MIT. [_Technology used: WebAssembly, WebCodecs, service worker. Source on GitHub._](https://github.com/keenanlk/stayput)
