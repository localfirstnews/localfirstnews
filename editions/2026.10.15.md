## 🛠️ Tools & Resources

### [Streamgres](https://github.com/juspay/streamgres#readme)
"A subscription-native sync engine for PostgreSQL. Clients subscribe to SQL queries over a WebSocket and keep receiving the delta: the rows that entered, left or changed in their result, as soon as the change commits. No polling, no re-running queries."

### [Jevish: Like Jev, but local-first.](https://hemanth.github.io/jevish/)
"jevish behaves like TypeSafe's Jev model: single-pass semantic pattern matching, calibrated confidence logits, and zero prompt engineering. But it is not fully Jev—it is designed to run 100% in local CPU cache (<0.05ms, zero dependencies, offline). And when you need 99–100% frontier accuracy, it can make use of Jev when needed via speculative cascade."

### [TheYgent](https://theygent.ai/)
"A no-code, local-first studio for building and running AI agents. Inference runs on your machine — llama.cpp, MLX, or vLLM — or any OpenAI-compatible API you register"

### [supabase-offline-kt](https://nesfeder.github.io/supabase-offline-kt/)
"supabase-offline-kt is a plugin for supabase-kt that keeps a Kotlin Multiplatform app working without a network, on Android and iOS."
