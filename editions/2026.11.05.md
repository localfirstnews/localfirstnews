### [AutoDoc](https://getautodoc.com/go-localfirstnews?utm_source=localfirstnews&utm_medium=editorial&utm_campaign=autodoc-growth)
Free, open-source meeting notes for Mac and Windows that also record your screen, so you can click a note and replay that moment. Transcription, notes and Ask AI run on your computer, recordings are encrypted at rest, and there is no account and no meeting bot. Version 1.3.3 (Oct 30) adds a 27-language interface and dark mode. _Electron + React; on-device transcription and local Ollama for notes; AES-256-GCM encrypted local library; no sync server._
