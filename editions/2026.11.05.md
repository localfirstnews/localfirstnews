## ✨ Apps
### [TapScribe](https://tapscribe.app/)
Mac app that records your mic and the Mac's audio from any meeting app, with no bot joining, and transcribes on-device. It also transcribes audio and video files. Transcripts sit next to the audio as plain local files and export as Markdown. _On-device transcription, local files._
