## 📰 News & Discussions 

### [Towards a Local-First Desktop | Julian Sparber & Tobias Bernard @ GUADEC 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnF9NzBidPA)
"This talk introduces the newly formed Modal Collective, and the ongoing effort to bring local-first sync to the GNOME ecosystem. So far, this has mostly been focused around Reflection, a local-first collaborative notes app, easier APIs for third party apps, and GObject bindings for p2panda. This year it's being expanded with a prototype for a system sync service, which any app can use to easily get peer-to-peer connectivity. Julian and Tobias explain all the moving parts required to build an app with local-first collaboration, how it can be done today, what needs further work, and how you can get involved!"

### [LFM2.5-2.6B: Deploy Agents Everywhere](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-2-6b)
"Today, we release LFM2.5-2.6B, an agentic model that runs entirely on-device. It is small enough to run on a phone, fast enough to stay responsive on a CPU, and capable enough to power agentic workflows: planning, calling tools, and tackling multi-step tasks. Unlike agents that depend on cloud APIs, local agents give you free inference, low latency, and real privacy."

### [Unleashing On-Device AI with LiteRT and Flutter - Lu Wang & Erin Walsh | Fluttercon USA 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oIarKo1A4DY)
"Discover how to supercharge your Flutter apps with on-device AI using LiteRT! In this talk, Lu Wang and Erin Walsh demonstrate how to deploy on-device AI models seamlessly across multiple platforms, taking advantage of hardware acceleration for maximum efficiency."

### [Electric is joining Databricks](https://electric.ax/blog/2026/08/11/electric-joining-databricks)
