## 🛠️ Tools & Resources

### [Rowboat: Your AI coworker, with memory](https://www.rowboatlabs.com/)
"Rowboat is local-first: data is stored as plain Markdown files you can read, edit, or delete anytime. It is Apache-2.0 and works with any LLM, including local models through Ollama or LM Studio." See also [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48819808)

### [Rindle: reactive queries, anywhere.](https://rindle.sh/)
"Register a query once and keep it's results up to date whenever data changes, never re-running the full query again."

### [Atlas: Local-first repository intelligence for AI-assisted engineering](https://atlas-repo-chi.vercel.app/)
"Scan once, on your machine. Atlas maps your architecture, dependency graph, risk and impact — then hands Cursor, Claude and Codex evidence-backed context on demand, instead of letting them guess."


## ✨ Apps

### [One Raven: A Truly Private Smart Home](https://oneraven.com/)
"Most smart homes rely on the cloud. This one doesn't. With One Raven, everything runs right inside your home, so what you do stays yours."

### [Muesli](https://muesli.works/)
"Muesli is an open-source Mac app for local speech-to-text, AI dictation, and meeting transcription. Speak naturally, paste clean text, and keep transcripts close instead of renting out speech to the cloud."

### [Bramble: Local-first, encrypted password manager](https://github.com/flythenimbus/bramble#readme)
