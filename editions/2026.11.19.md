## ✨ Apps

### [Estimate Cam](https://estimatecam.com)
Free, MIT-licensed browser app for solo contractors to organize job photos, prepare scopes and estimates, and export branded reports. Projects use local IndexedDB storage with backup/export; no signup or device sync. Optional AI uses the user's provider, and browser dictation may send audio to its speech provider. [Source](https://github.com/zeds88/Estimatecam.app/tree/main/jobcam). _React/TypeScript PWA, IndexedDB._
