# 2026.10.29

## 📰 News & Discussions

### [Present your work at upcoming FOSDEM conference](https://openlocalfirst.org/)
[FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/2026/) (biggest Free and Open Source conference in Europe) will host, for the second time this year, a **devroom/track** specialized in open source **Local First** software! You are all welcome to [propose your talk, demo, keynote as the CfP is now open, for 40 days](https://openlocalfirst.org/). The program will then be announced here too. Looking forward meeting you all in Brussels at the end of January 2027 for a full-day conference. [Last year was a real success!](https://archive.fosdem.org/2026/schedule/track/local-first/).
