### [How Local-First Sync Handles Offline Changes](https://donebear.com/blog/server-sequenced-sync-offline-changes)

Done Bear's engineering write-up explains a production sync loop built around a durable outbox, server-assigned action ordering, idempotent mutation identities, WebSocket buffering during HTTP catch-up, and full bootstrap for stale cursors. It also states the server-wins conflict trade-off instead of claiming CRDT semantics.

### [Me.: your health data becomes an actionable profile of you](https://meapp.health/)
"Me. brings your health data together, lets you add the context only you know, including blood results, medications, supplements and notes on the things that show up in no number at all, and turns it into one document you can hand to a GP, a coach or an AI. It won't score you or tell you what to do. It builds the picture, you choose who reads or analyses it." "There is no Me. account and no Me. server. Your data stays on your device, with an optional iCloud backup you control. Anonymous analytics through TelemetryDeck, disclosed in-app with an opt-out."
