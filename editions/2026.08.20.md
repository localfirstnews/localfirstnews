### [How Local-First Sync Handles Offline Changes](https://donebear.com/blog/server-sequenced-sync-offline-changes)

Done Bear's engineering write-up explains a production sync loop built around a durable outbox, server-assigned action ordering, idempotent mutation identities, WebSocket buffering during HTTP catch-up, and full bootstrap for stale cursors. It also states the server-wins conflict trade-off instead of claiming CRDT semantics.
