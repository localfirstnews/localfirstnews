## ✨ Apps

### [Postern](https://www.getpostern.com/#setup)
A self-hosted, open-source gateway that connects your money, health, mail, calendar, contacts, and home to any AI agent you allow, through one MCP surface. Agents read from a local cache or fetch live from the source, never see your credentials, and work under revocable per-sector grants with every access logged. There is no LLM inside.
