## 📰 News & Discussions

### [Propose a talk at FOSDEM conference](https://openlocalfirst.org/)
[FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/2026/) (biggest Free and Open Source conference in Europe) will host, for the second time this year, a **devroom/track** specialized in open source **Local First** software! There is one week left before the deadline to [submit your talk!](https://openlocalfirst.org/). The program will then be announced here. The conference is at ULB in Brussels on Sunday 31 January 2027 for the whole day. [See last last year's program](https://archive.fosdem.org/2026/schedule/track/local-first/)

## ✨ Apps

### [Zella](https://zellahq.com/)
Screen recorder and AI video editor for Mac and iPhone. Recording, editing and export run on the device and projects stay in local files; there is no account and the video is never uploaded. Two optional features use the network: an AI Assistant with your own API key, and stock B-roll. _Native Swift apps (AppKit and SwiftUI), local files, on-device processing._
