## ✨ Apps

### [Zella](https://zellahq.com/)
Screen recorder and AI video editor for Mac and iPhone. Recording, editing and export run on the device and projects stay in local files; there is no account and the video is never uploaded. Two optional features use the network: an AI Assistant with your own API key, and stock B-roll. _Native Swift apps (AppKit and SwiftUI), local files, on-device processing._

### [Codex Desk](https://github.com/blucca/codex-desk)
Free, MIT-licensed terminal-launched browser app for finding past Codex answers and reading original conversations, command output and tool evidence. Explicitly index recent native history and search a remembered phrase to inspect its original context. Cached history and drafts persist in local SQLite; optional same-thread continuation uses your configured Codex provider. [Try the labeled synthetic sample](https://blucca.github.io/codex-desk/demo/) or launch with `uvx --prerelease allow codex-desk` for your own history.

### [Linger](https://withlinger.com/)
Linger is a local-first macOS image organizer for design references. Its native rewrite adds project canvases, and the library keeps images, source links, and notes on the Mac for offline access.
