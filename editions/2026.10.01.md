### [Governed Crossing: draft AT Protocol lexicons for recording what leaves a local-first system](https://github.com/jediwright/governedcrossing)
When data, or access to it, crosses from a person's own local-first system into shared infrastructure, Governed Crossing records it as a signed crossing record kept by that person: what was authorized to cross, by whom, when, and where it landed. The work grew out of a prototype that gates publishing from a Keyhive-protected Automerge document to Bluesky with intent and completion records ([featured in This Month in Automerge, August](https://automerge.org/blog/2026-august/)). A new [draft on recording access changes](https://github.com/jediwright/governedcrossing/blob/main/drafts/access-change.md) has five open questions in the repo's GitHub Discussions through Oct 26.
