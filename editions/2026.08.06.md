### [NoteGen v0.33.0](https://github.com/codexu/note-gen/releases/tag/note-gen-v0.33.0)
NoteGen, an open-source local-first Markdown notes app, adds an editable canvas workspace for diagrams, mind maps, timelines, drawings, and charts. The release also brings canvas editing to mobile, improves file management and sync behavior, and expands AI-assisted retrieval while keeping written notes as ordinary files in a user-selected workspace.
