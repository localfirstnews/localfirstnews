## 🛠️ Tools & Resources

### [Litelink](https://github.com/nhobin219/litelink#readme)
"[A] local-first, embedded capture library I built in Python (code is heavily AI generated but designed and reviewed by yours truly). I've been using this for point-and-shoot WebSocket capture but I imagine it could also be useful for observability/metrics ingestion as well. Litelink supports a single writer per stream.
I've been doing a lot of development and deployments on tiny VMs (2 vCPU, 8GB, 50-100GB disk) and didn't want the complexity or cost of managing central brokers (Kafka), databases (Postgres), and CDC/connectors just to get queryable WebSocket stream capture running."

### [Cbox Sync for Laravel](https://github.com/cboxdk/laravel-sync#readme)
"Offline-first sync for Laravel applications: one authoritative server, many replicas, field-level conflicts that are preserved rather than silently overwritten."
