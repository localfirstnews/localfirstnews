Conflict Tolerance for Local-First Collaboration. Jonas Schürmann, Local-First Conf 2026, Lab Day, Berlin.

### [The Path to Sovereign Data: Challenges and Priorities in Local-First Computing](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/07/data-ownership-localfirst/)
"Local-first computing advocates argued that 'data ownership' had to mean practical user power, interoperable infrastructure, and governance models beyond individual account control, during a panel at Local First Conf 2026 in Berlin titled Data Ownership Beyond Local-First."

## 🛠️ Tools & Resources

### [Brainstorm](https://getbrainstorm.online)
"Local-first open-source end-to-end encrypted knowledge management OS."

### [PairDrop: P2P local file transfer based on WebRTC](https://github.com/schlagmichdoch/pairdrop#readme)
"File sharing on your local network that works on all platforms. A multi-platform AirDrop-like solution that works. Send images, documents or text via peer-to-peer connection to devices on the same local network." [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48927900)

### [WebBrain: The Open-Source AI Browser Agent](https://github.com/webbrain-one/webbrain#readme)
"Your data stays yours. Run with a local LLM for zero data leakage. No telemetry, no tracking, no accounts required. Fully open-source."


## ✨ Apps

### [Handy: speak into any text field](https://handy.computer/)
"The free and open source app for speech to text" "Your voice stays on your computer. Get transcriptions without sending audio to the cloud."

### [RPGUni](https://rpguni.com/)
"RPGUni is a free, local-first desktop app for worldbuilding and running tabletop RPGs. Craft universes of characters, places, and lore that link together, then play in them. Everything lives on your machine."

### [Syedello](https://www.syedello.com/about)
"Syedello is a free local-first project command center for project managers, freelancers, small teams, founders, agencies, NGOs, students, and delivery leads who need real control without server lock-in. Track sprint velocity, baseline Gantt charts, and RAID logs instantly with absolute data privacy."
